What the numbers mean

About this map

Where the numbers come from. Each area has a Raspberry Pi with a calibrated microphone. It measures the A-weighted sound level (dB) and uses AI to recognize the main source of noise, such as traffic, people or machinery. No audio recordings are published.

Livability grade. Grades use the day-evening-night level (Lden) of the last 24 hours, which counts evening noise a bit more and night noise even more, because noise hurts most when people rest. A is under 55 dB, B 55–60, C 60–65, D 65–70 and E 70 dB or more.

Health guidance. The WHO recommends keeping outdoor noise below 55 dB during the day and 40 dB at night. Long-term exposure above these levels is linked to poor sleep, stress and heart problems.

For local governments and agencies. LGU, DENR and DOH staff can sign in for the full dashboard, forecasts and a planning report.
